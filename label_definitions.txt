assertion_reaction: an opinion, judgment, hot take, or reaction that offers no checkable support
evidenced_claim: a claim backed by at least one explicit figure (a stat value, fee, age, league position, or contract term) that directly supports it
inquiry: an open question seeking information, advice, or the community's opinions, without asserting the author's own answer
